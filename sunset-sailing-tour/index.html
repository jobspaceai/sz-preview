Sunset Sailing

The most famous sunset in the Adriatic, seen from the water.

Duration 3 hours
Departure 18:00
Group size 10 max
From Marina Zadar

Alfred Hitchcock called Zadar's sunset the most beautiful in the world, and he saw it from the shore. You will see it from a sailboat in the middle of the channel, with a glass of local wine and the Sea Organ playing behind you. Three hours, zero effort, one unforgettable evening.

  • Sail past the Sea Organ and Greeting to the Sun
  • Local wine and soft drinks included
  • Small groups, max 10 guests
  • Perfect for proposals and anniversaries

How the day unfolds

18:00

Depart Marina Zadar

Evening breeze, sails up, city walls sliding past.

18:45

The Sea Organ from the water

We sail slowly past Zadar’s famous sound sculpture. You hear it best from the sea.

19:30

Golden hour in the channel

Anchored or under easy sail, wine poured, cameras out.

20:15

Sunset

The sun drops behind Ugljan. This is the moment everyone came for.

21:00

Return to the marina

A slow night sail back under the city lights.

From the boat

Good to know

Is this tour romantic or party-style?

Romantic and calm. Small groups, quiet music if any, and the sunset as the main event. For celebrations, book it private.

Can you help me plan a proposal?

Yes, and we are good at it. Tell us in the booking note and the skipper will arrange the moment discreetly.
