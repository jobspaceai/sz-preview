2. What Data We Collect

We only collect personal data that is necessary to provide our services and respond to your inquiries. This includes:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Information related to your inquiry (interest, type of sailing, dates, number of people, message)

3. How We Use Your Data

Your personal data is used exclusively for:

  • responding to inquiries and contact requests
  • preparing and sending personalized offers
  • managing reservations and bookings
  • customer communication regarding our services

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Payment Information

For reservation payments, we use Stripe, a secure third-party payment processor.

  • Payments are processed directly through Stripe
  • We do not store your card details
  • Only necessary booking information is retained

For more information, please review Stripe's privacy policy.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data based on:

  • your consent (when submitting the contact form)
  • performance of a service (booking and communication)
  • legal obligations (if applicable)

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary:

  • to fulfill your request or booking
  • to comply with legal obligations

7. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to improve user experience and website functionality. Cookies help us:

  • understand how visitors use the website
  • improve content and performance

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. We may use tools such as Google Analytics (for website usage tracking).

8. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access your personal data
  • request correction or deletion
  • restrict processing
  • withdraw consent

To exercise your rights, contact us at info@sailingzadar.com.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. All changes will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at info@sailingzadar.com.
