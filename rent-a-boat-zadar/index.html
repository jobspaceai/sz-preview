Good to know

Do I need a licence to rent a boat?

For bare rental, yes: a valid boating licence. No licence? Take the skippered option and we drive.

Is fuel included in the rental price?

No, fuel is charged by use, and a refundable deposit may apply. Everything is agreed clearly before you take the boat.
