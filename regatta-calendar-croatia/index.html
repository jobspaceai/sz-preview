Working calendar, dates move

This is a transcription of the Croatian Sailing Federation working calendar, taken from hjs.hr on 9 August 2026. Dates get moved and events get cancelled during the season. Check with the organiser, or against the official calendar on hjs.hr, before you plan anything around a date on this page.

The racing season is spring and autumn, not summer

Counted by month, the 110 races in our area fall like this: April 14, May 29, June 13, July 3, August none at all, September 13, October 21. August is the only month in the year without a single race between Zadar, Biograd and Šibenik.

August is also the month when every boat trip is full and no boat is free. May and October are the exact opposite: almost no tourism, and 50 races between them. That is not an argument, it is a sum from the calendar.

The reason is the wind, and sailors know it already. July and August run on the afternoon thermal, which fills in around lunchtime, blows for a few hours and dies. In May and October real weather systems come through, the wind holds for longer parts of the day, and a race course means something.

107 of the 110 races here run over more than one day. Multi day events are mostly charter regattas, which is to say events where crews arrive by plane and need a boat and a berth once they get here. That is the part of this calendar that concerns us directly.
