The fleet

Three sailing yachts and two speedboats, owned and maintained by us. The boat you book is the boat you get.

Dufour 460 GL "Black Ray"

Year2018
Length14.15 m
Beam4.50 m
Cabins4
Bathrooms4
EngineYanmar 54 HP
Day guests12

Our flagship and the boat most guests meet first. Black Ray is a modern Dufour 460 Grand Large from 2018: 14.15 metres of hull, a wide 4.5-metre beam, and the kind of deck space that makes a full-day tour feel uncrowded even with a big group. Below deck she sleeps eight across four double cabins, each with its own private bathroom, a rarity in this class and the reason she carries our 3-day trips and gourmet charters. The galley is fully equipped, there is warm water throughout, and the navigation fit-out (GPS, autopilot, VHF) means the skipper always has one eye on comfort and both on safety.

Salona 44 "Giordano Bruno"

Year2011
Length13.50 m
Beam4.20 m
Cabins4
Bathrooms2
Engineinboard diesel
Day guests10

A performance cruiser with a Croatian pedigree: Salona yachts are built in Solin for exactly this sea. Giordano Bruno measures 13.5 metres with a 4.2-metre beam, four cabins and two bathrooms, and she is the boat we untie when guests want to feel real sailing. Built for both speed and stability, she comes alive when the maestral fills in; taking the helm on a beam reach here is the moment many guests remember longest. A functional galley and full navigation equipment make her equally at home on day trips and longer Adriatic passages.

Jeanneau Sun Odyssey 36i "Lovejoy"

Year2008
Length10.94 m
Beam3.60 m
Cabins3
Bathrooms1
Engineinboard diesel
Day guests8

Friendly, forgiving and easy to learn on, Lovejoy is the home of our sailing school and the favourite of smaller private groups. At 10.94 metres with a 3.6-metre beam, three cabins and one bathroom, she offers a practical layout combined with smooth sailing performance: light on the helm, quick to tack, and stable enough that first-timers relax within the hour. By Sunday evening of a weekend course, students are sailing her back into Marina Zadar themselves.
