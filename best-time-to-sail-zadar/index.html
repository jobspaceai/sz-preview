When to Sail in Zadar: a Month-by-Month Guide

The Zadar sailing season runs from May to October, and the months are not interchangeable. Here is what each one actually gives you.

May: cool sea, empty bays

The land is warm, the sea is not quite there yet, usually still in the high teens. Everything else is at its best: the islands are green, the bays are empty, the light is clear and prices are lower. Good for sailing, sightseeing and photography; less good if the point of the day is swimming for hours.

June: the sweet spot

Sea temperature catches up, days are at their longest, and the crowds have not arrived. The maestral, the afternoon thermal wind that makes sailing on this coast reliable, settles into its summer pattern. If you want the postcard version of the archipelago without sharing it, this is the month.

July and August: hot, busy, alive

The warmest sea, the strongest sun, the fullest anchorages and the busiest town. It is also when the archipelago has its own life: Saljske užance in Sali on the first weekend of August, konobas open, everything running. Book earlier than you think you need to, start earlier in the day than you would like, and treat the middle of the day as swimming time rather than sightseeing time.

Practical note: in these two months, Sakarun before eleven or after four, not in between.

September: the local favourite

The sea is at its warmest of the whole year, having spent three months absorbing heat, while the crowds thin out from the first week onward. The wind often becomes more interesting for actual sailing. Almost everyone in this business, asked privately when they would take their own holiday here, says September.

October: the long goodbye

Warm sea, soft light, quiet bays, and a rising chance that the weather has opinions. Trips still run, but the forecast starts to steer the schedule rather than decorate it. If your dates are flexible and you do not mind a change of plan, October gives you the archipelago almost to yourself.

A word about wind

Whether we sail or motor depends on the wind, in every month. The maestral fills in on summer afternoons and is a gift; the bora comes down cold and hard from the mainland and is a reason to change plans; the jugo brings cloud and swell. The skipper decides on the day, always in favour of safety, and if the weather makes a trip unsafe we reschedule free of charge or refund in full.

Pick your month, then let the sea have the last word. It will anyway.
