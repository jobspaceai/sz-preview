How much does a boat tour in Zadar cost?

Shared sailing tours run 50 to 120 EUR per person depending on length; private boats start at 250 EUR (sunset) to 800 EUR (full day). Speedboat rental is 200 to 300 EUR per day. Every price is on the tour page, with no hidden fees.

Sailboat or speedboat: which should I pick?

Sailboat for the experience: swimming, lunch aboard, a slow day between islands. Speedboat for distance: Sakarun, Dugi Otok and the outer islands in a single day. If it is your first time, most guests start with the day sailing trip.

Where do the tours leave from?

All tours depart from Marina Zadar, Obala kneza Branimira 1, a short walk from the old town. Meeting details arrive with your booking confirmation.

Can we book a boat just for our group?

Yes. Every tour has a private option where the whole boat is yours and the route bends to your wishes, including birthdays and celebrations aboard.
