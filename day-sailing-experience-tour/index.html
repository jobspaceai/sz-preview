Day Sailing Experience

Eight hours of islands, open sails and long swims.

Duration 8 hours
Departure 10:00
Group size 12 max
From Marina Zadar

This is the tour our guests talk about for years. You board at Marina Zadar, the skipper sets the sails, and the day follows a route we have polished over many seasons: the Tri Sestrice islets first, then a surprise stop when wind and sea allow, the old olive port of Muline, and the quiet bay of Frnaža for the last long swim. On a private charter, the route bends entirely to your wishes.

  • The route: Tri Sestrice, Muline and Frnaža bay
  • One surprise stop when conditions allow
  • Light lunch, water and beer included
  • SUP and snorkeling gear on board
  • Private charter: route tailored to your wishes

How the day unfolds

10:00

Cast off from Marina Zadar

Welcome aboard, safety briefing, sails up as we clear the breakwater.

11:00

Tri Sestrice islets

First stop: the Three Sisters off Rivanj, the closest thing the channel has to an aquarium. Swim, snorkel, stare.

12:30

The surprise stop

If wind and sea agree, the skipper adds a stop that stays off the brochure. Every crew gets a different one.

13:30

Lunch at anchor off Muline

A light lunch aboard, usually a pasta salad with meat, with local wine, beer or juice, anchored by the old olive port of Muline.

15:30

Frnaža bay

The last long swim: pebbles, pines and water clear to the anchor chain.

17:00

Return sail

Downwind run back toward the city with the Velebit mountains behind you.

18:00

Dock at Marina Zadar

Back in the marina, salty and happy.

Before you board

  • Bring at least 1 litre of water per person. There is water aboard, but eight hours of summer sun ask for more.
  • Lunch is a light meal, usually a pasta salad with meat. If you are a bigger eater, or particular about food, pack something extra.
  • Sun and wind protection are not optional: hat, sunscreen, and a layer against the wind. Conditions at sea change quickly.

From the boat

Good to know

Do I need sailing experience?

None at all. The skipper handles the boat; you can help with the sails if you want to learn, or simply relax.

Can we go to Sakarun beach?

Not on the day trip; under sail Sakarun needs more time. It is part of the 3-day route, and our speedboats reach it in a day.

What happens in bad weather?

Safety comes first. If the forecast is bad we reschedule for free or refund your deposit in full.
