Boat Tour Prices in Zadar: What You Pay, and What You Get

Boat trip prices in Zadar look confusing because two different things are being priced: a seat, or a boat. Once you know which one you are looking at, the whole market becomes readable in about a minute. What follows is our own pricelist, with the arithmetic that decides which option costs you less.

Per person or per boat, the only distinction that matters

A group tour sells you one place on a boat that other people are also on. You pay per head, you meet strangers, and the route is fixed because it has to work for everyone aboard.

A private charter sells you the whole boat. You pay one number regardless of whether there are two of you or ten, nobody else comes, and the route bends to what you feel like doing. Neither is better. They are answers to different questions, and the price gap between them closes faster than most people expect.

What a place on a shared tour costs

Three prices cover almost everything we do. A sunset sail is 50 EUR per person for three hours. A half day is 75 EUR for four hours with two swim stops. A full day is 120 EUR for eight hours, and that one includes lunch, water and beer, plus snorkelling gear and a paddleboard.

The full day is the one that surprises people. Eight hours, food and drink aboard, and a route that runs out to the Tri Sestrice islets, the old olive harbour at Muline and a last long swim in Frnaža bay. Per hour on the water it is the cheapest thing on this list.

What a whole boat costs

Private charter starts at 250 EUR for a sunset, 400 EUR for a half day and 800 EUR for a full day. Those are boat prices. Twelve people pay the same 800 EUR as two people do.

The arithmetic nobody publishes

Divide the private price by the per person price and you get the number of guests at which private becomes the cheaper option. It is worth doing before you book.

For the sunset, 250 divided by 50 is five. At five people a private boat costs the same as five shared places, and from the sixth person on you are paying less and getting the boat to yourselves. For the half day, 400 divided by 75 is a little over five, so six people. For the full day, 800 divided by 120 is under seven, so seven people.

In plain terms: a couple should book a shared place, a family of four usually should too, and any group from six upward should ask for the private price first. We would rather tell you that than sell you six separate seats.

Three days, and why it is priced per cabin

The three day trip to Kornati and Telašćica is 880 EUR per cabin, and a cabin sleeps two. That is neither a per person price nor a whole boat price, and there is a reason: you are buying somewhere to sleep, not somewhere to sit.

Two people in a cabin pay 440 each for three days, two nights, a skipper and a route through Kornati National Park and Telašćica Nature Park. Whole boat charter for the same three days is available on request.

The gourmet day, and the speedboats

The gourmet sailing day is 1200 EUR for two guests and 1400 EUR for four, with a cook aboard and a tasting menu with local wines. It is the one product where the boat is genuinely a private restaurant that happens to move.

Speedboat rental runs 200 to 300 EUR a day depending on the boat and the season, and fuel is not included in that. If you hold a boat licence you can take it out yourself. If you do not, we send a skipper with it.

What is included, and the three things that are not

Included in every tour price: the skipper, the fuel, the boat, safety equipment and the port fee. That last one matters, because it is charged separately by some operators and appears at the pier when you have already committed. Ours is in the number you see, and there is nothing to pay on board.

Not included, honestly: fuel on a bareboat speedboat rental, food and drink on the weekend sailing course along with mooring fees, and any national park entry ticket where the route enters one. On the day tours, lunch and drinks are in the price.

Booking, deposits and getting your money back

You do not need a credit card to reserve a date. If the forecast is bad we reschedule at no cost or refund your deposit in full, because taking a boat out in weather it should not be out in is not a decision we let a deposit make for us. For cancellations that are not weather, rescheduling or a refund is free up to seven days before departure.

If you are still deciding which trip fits, the month by month guide is more useful than the price list, and the overview of all tours puts them side by side. If you would rather just ask, tell us how many of you there are and which date, and we will tell you which of the two prices is the lower one for your group.
