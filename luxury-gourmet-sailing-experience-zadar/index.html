Luxury Gourmet Sailing

A private yacht, a set menu, and nowhere you have to be.

Duration 8 hours
Departure Flexible
Group size 8 max
From Marina Zadar

Our most indulgent day on the water. The yacht is exclusively yours, the route is built around your wishes, and the galley turns out a multi-course menu of Dalmatian produce: fresh fish, local olive oil, island cheeses, paired wines. It is a restaurant table that happens to sail through a national park.

  • Whole yacht exclusively yours
  • Multi-course Dalmatian tasting menu
  • Paired local wines and champagne welcome
  • Route fully tailored to you

How the day unfolds

Morning

First courses at anchor

Main courses served slowly, swims between them. Nobody is in a hurry.

Dessert on the return sail, sunset over the archipelago.
