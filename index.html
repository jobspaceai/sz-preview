Questions we hear on the dock

Is Croatia good for sailing?

The Zadar archipelago is one of Europe’s best sailing grounds: over 300 islands, protected channels, steady summer breezes and two national or nature parks within a day’s sail.

Do I pay online?

No. You send a booking request, we confirm availability within about 2 hours, and you pay on board or by bank transfer. No credit card needed to book.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free rescheduling or full refund for weather cancellations. For other cancellations, free up to 7 days before departure.

When is the best time to sail in Zadar?

May to October. July and August are warmest and busiest; June and September give you the same sea with quieter bays.

Can children join the tours?

Absolutely. We carry child-size life jackets and our skippers are used to young crew. Tell us the ages in the booking note.
