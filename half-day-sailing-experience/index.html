Half-Day Sailing

Four hours on the water. Fits any holiday schedule.

Duration 4 hours
Departure 09:00 or 14:00
Group size 12 max
From Marina Zadar

Short on time, not on appetite for the sea? The half-day trip is four hours built around swimming: two stops chosen on the day from Galevac, Ošljak, Otrić or Kostanj bay, depending on weather and wind. How much we actually sail depends on the wind; the swimming is guaranteed either way.

  • Two swim stops, picked from four spots by the wind
  • Galevac, Ošljak, Otrić or Kostanj bay
  • Water and beer on board
  • Great first sailing experience for families

How the day unfolds

0:00

Departure

Cast off from Marina Zadar. If the wind plays along, sails go up right away.

0:45

First swim stop

Usually Galevac or Ošljak, the little islands off Preko. Anchor down, everyone in.

2:00

Second swim stop

Otrić or Kostanj bay, whichever the wind favours. Snorkeling and a cold drink.

3:15

Return

Back across the channel, under sail if the wind serves.

4:00

Back at the marina

You still have half a day of holiday left.

What's included

Skipper
Water & beer
Snorkeling gear
Fuel & marina fees
Meals: bring your own snacks

Before you board

  • No meal on this trip: water and beer are aboard, everything else you bring yourself.
  • Sailing depends on the wind. If there is none, or it is unfavourable, we motor between the bays; the swim stops stay.
  • Even on four hours: sunscreen, a hat and a light layer against the wind. The sea changes conditions fast.

Where we depart from

Marina Zadar, Ul. Ivana Meštrovića 3, 23000 Zadar · Departure: 09:00 or 14:00

The berth is on the main pier of Marina Zadar, a 15-minute walk from the Sea Organ.

From the boat

Good to know

Morning or afternoon: which is better?

Morning is calmer and cooler, great with kids. Afternoon has livelier wind and warmer sea. Both get two swim stops.

Will we actually sail?

If there is usable wind, yes, and gladly. With no wind or the wrong wind we motor; on a four-hour trip we do not chase it at the cost of your swimming time.
