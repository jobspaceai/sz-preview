Sailing School: Weekend Course

Arrive a passenger. Leave part of the crew.

Duration Weekend (2-3 days)
Departure Sat 09:00
Group size 6 max
From Marina Zadar

A weekend that changes how you see the sea. Aboard our Sun Odyssey 36i you learn the fundamentals: points of sail, winch work, basic navigation, anchoring, and how a crew actually works together. By Sunday evening you are tacking the boat yourself.

  • Hands-on from the first hour
  • Max 6 students per boat
  • Sleep aboard: full liveaboard weekend
  • Foundation for skipper licence training

How the day unfolds

Sat AM

Boat systems & first sail

Safety, lines, sail trim basics. You leave the marina as crew, not cargo.

Sat PM

Points of sail & anchoring

Practice tacks and jibes in open water, then anchor for the night in a bay.

Sun AM

Navigation & manoeuvres

Chart basics, man-overboard drill, harbour manoeuvres.

Sun PM

Your passage home

The students sail the boat back to Zadar. Instructor keeps hands in pockets.

From the boat

Good to know

Do I get a licence?

The weekend course is practical training, not a licence exam. It is the ideal foundation before a licence course, and we can point you to the next step.

What if I know nothing about boats?

Perfect. The course assumes zero knowledge and builds up from the ropes, literally.
