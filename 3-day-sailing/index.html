3-Day Sailing: Kornati & Telašćica

Three days. Two parks. One boat that becomes home.

Duration 3 days / 2 nights
Departure Day 1, 09:00
Group size 8 max
From Marina Zadar

This is the real thing: a small liveaboard expedition through the two most protected seascapes of the Adriatic. You sail through the stone labyrinth of Kornati National Park, anchor overnight in Telašćica bay, swim in the salt lake, and eat dinner in a konoba only reachable by boat. Ninety islands in three days, and the boat is your hotel.

  • Kornati National Park under sail
  • Overnight at anchor in Telašćica
  • Salt lake Mir swim stop
  • Dinners in boat-only konobas
  • All meals aboard prepared by your skipper

How the day unfolds

Day 1

Zadar to Kornati

Morning departure, sail south through Dugi Otok channel, first night at anchor inside the national park.

Day 2

Kornati labyrinth & Telašćica

Island-hopping through the archipelago, cliffs of Dugi Otok, swim in the salt lake, konoba dinner in the bay.

Day 3

Sakarun & return

Morning at Sakarun’s white-sand beach, then the long, beautiful sail home to Zadar.

Marina Zadar, Ul. Ivana Meštrovića 3, 23000 Zadar · Departure: Day 1, 09:00

From the boat

Good to know

Where do we sleep?

Aboard the yacht, in a private double cabin. The boat anchors in protected bays; nights are calm and unforgettably quiet.

Is it suitable for beginners?

Completely. Many guests have never sailed before. The skipper handles the boat and teaches anyone who is curious.
